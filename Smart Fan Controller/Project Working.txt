1. When the NodeMCU starts, it initializes the buttons, relay, OLED display and serial communication.
2. The OLED displays System Ready.
3. The user presses one of the timer buttons.
4. The NodeMCU switches ON the relay and starts the selected countdown.
5. The OLED continuously displays the remaining time in HH:MM:SS format
6. When the countdown reaches zero, the relay switches OFF automatically.
7. In Continuous Mode, the fan stays ON until the OFF button is pressed.

Feature	Function

1. 4-hour timer	Fan runs for 4 hours, then switches OFF
2. 5-hour timer	Fan runs for 5 hours, then switches OFF
3. 6-hour timer	Fan runs for 6 hours, then switches OFF
4. Continuous ON	Fan remains ON until manually stopped
5. Manual OFF	Immediately switches the fan OFF
6. OLED display	Displays operating mode and remaining time
7. Automatic shutdown	Relay switches OFF when the selected timer expires
